Reading Reflections

Personal Reflection on Nataliya Kosmyna's "Your Brain on ChatGPT" and AI in educational settings

Your Brain on ChatGPT, an article by Nataliya Kosmyna from the MIT Media Lab publication discusses findings from their study on the “cognitive cost of using an LLM in the educational context of writing an essay.” The researchers conducted this study by comparing essays written by ‘brain-only groups’ and LMM assisted writing groups. Reading about the results was very eye-opening, especially because of how new and fast this technology actually is, and how little studies and limitations have been done on it. LMM products like ChatGPT are new tools that were made to help us, but are now taking over more and more parts of human life in an alarmingly fast way. The study examines the results of “a possible decrease in learning skills.” This stood out to me because it can affect each one of us. The study goes on to talk about how the LLM participant group performed the worst in the “brain-only group at all levels.” These results not only show us how the LMM group scored the lowest amongst the other groups, but also how alarmingly weak their brain engagement is. The study should be a warning to everyone long term, weak writing skills and brain engagement can affect how we think. Whether it is thinking critically in a classroom or just being able to gather our own thoughts. After reading the article I have even more questions and concerns about our future. The article does point out there were some limitations in the study such as a limited number of participants, and it focused ‘on writing an essay in an educational setting and may not generalize across tasks’. But this research alone should be a waving red flag to all. Being in school throughout the rise of AI has been an experience in itself. It almost feels like everything changed in the blink of an eye and everyone is just trying to keep up. It started with everything being online rather than on paper, then came AI and teachers' rules started to shift more and more. Programs like ‘Lock Down Browser’ are now being downloaded on all school computers to avoid cheating on online exams. But neither teachers nor students knew how to download it on the first day. We're all trying to adjust together. The Youtube video linked, Brain Experts WARNING: Watch This Before Using ChatGPT Again! (Shocking New Discovery) discusses in depth how AI negatively impacts the brain. Dr Danial Amen and Steven Bartlett agree that “we are grossly underestimating the impacts it's going to have,” when examining AI. I think everyone should read this article and watch the video, not to scare the audience but rather to see how real this issue is NOW. This matters especially today, with such little knowledge and limitations on AI people should begin to take it upon themselves to use it safely and when appropriate. If not, we are all just guinea pigs waiting to see the impacts AI has on us later down the line.
